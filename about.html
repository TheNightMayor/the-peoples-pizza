О коллективе · About the collective

Made for
the gathering.

The people's pizza started with a borrowed oven, a folding table, and a belief that pizza tastes better when everyone gets a seat—and every worker gets a voice.

“The best part is watching a quiet corner turn into a room full of people—and one very confident cat.”

We’re a tiny, traveling pizza kitchen organized as a worker-owned cooperative. We make decisions together, share the work, and believe good food should bring people closer.

There’s no permanent dining room here. That’s the point. Every pop-up is a little different, shaped by the neighborhood, the season, and the people who show up.

Come for the pizza. Stay for the solidarity.
