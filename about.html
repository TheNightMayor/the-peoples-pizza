A little about us

Made for
the gathering.

The people's pizza started with a borrowed oven, a folding table, and a belief that pizza tastes better when everyone gets a seat.

“The best part is watching a quiet corner turn into a room full of people.”

We’re a tiny, traveling pizza kitchen built by friends who love feeding people. We take our time with the dough, source thoughtfully, and keep the menu unfussy so the focus stays on the table.

There’s no permanent dining room here. That’s the point. Every pop-up is a little different, shaped by the neighborhood, the season, and the people who show up.

Come for the pizza. Stay for the conversation.
